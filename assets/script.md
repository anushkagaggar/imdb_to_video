# 3 Idiots — Video Script

## [HOOK] (0s – 10s)

Imagine three friends, bound by a promise, united by their quest for knowledge, and driven by a desire for freedom. Their journey is a testament to the power of friendship and the unbreakable bonds that tie us together.

## [CONTEXT] (10s – 30s)

Set against the backdrop of India's prestigious Imperial College of Engineering, we meet Farhan Qureshi and Raju Rastogi, two friends who have been searching for their third companion, Rancho, for years. But little do they know, their friend has been hiding a secret that will change everything.

## [PLOT_TEASE] (30s – 90s)

As they embark on a series of hilarious misadventures, they encounter a familiar face - Chatur Ramalingam, now a successful businessman, who reminds them of a bet they had undertaken 10 years ago. But what started as a joke has now become a quest to uncover the truth about Rancho's disappearance.
