# 3 Idiots — Video Script

## [HOOK] (0s – 10s)

In the vibrant streets of India, a story unfolds of three friends, bound by an unbreakable bond, who embark on a journey of self-discovery and friendship that will change their lives forever.

## [CONTEXT] (10s – 30s)

Farhan Qureshi and Raju Rastogi, two friends who met in college, are determined to reunite with their dear friend Rancho, a free-spirited genius who inspired them to rethink their priorities in life. Their plan involves faking a stroke and a fake divorce, setting off a chain of hilarious events. Ten years have passed since they last saw each other, and a lot has changed. Their friend Chatur Ramalingam, once a timid student, is now a successful businessman, who reminds them of a bet they had undertaken years ago. The trio's reunion takes them on a wild ride, filled with laughter, music, and adventure.

## [PLOT_TEASE] (30s – 90s)

As they travel across the country, they recollect their college days, filled with memories of their eccentric professor, Viru Sahastrabudhe, and their hilarious antics. Along the way, they stumble upon a hidden treasure that sets them on a mission to uncover the truth about Rancho's disappearance. With every new revelation, their bond grows stronger, and they begin to question the true meaning of success and friendship. Will they be able to find Rancho, and will their friendship be able to withstand the test of time?

## [CTA] (90s – 120s)

Join Farhan, Raju, and Chatur on their unforgettable journey of self-discovery and friendship. With its heartwarming story, memorable characters, and stunning visuals, '3 Idiots' is a must-watch. Streaming now on major platforms, including Amazon Prime Video and Netflix.
